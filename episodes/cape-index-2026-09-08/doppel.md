# DOPPEL · entities and consistency

*Two records for the same soul, one row for two souls. Both hurt the count. Both are here.*

DOPPEL audited data/web-cast.json against data/cast-raw.json, data/cast-overrides.json, scripts/scrape-cast.mjs, and the cached Wikipedia Cast sections in data/raw/wikicast-*.txt for 91 films and 257 characters. Universe totals (MCU 38, Fox 17, SSU 13, DCEU 15, DCU 2, Elseworlds 6) tie out, every byFilm title matches a web.json film t, no character references a title outside the 91, no exact-name duplicates, and no actor is carried under two spellings inside any row. The findings below are all appearance-count drift, one merged pair, and the canonicalization bug that hides one of the overrides the file already tried to add.

## Critical

**1. Kraglin is credited in one film instead of at least four.** data/web-cast.json characters "Kraglin" carries only Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 3. Sean Gunn is credited as Kraglin Obfonteri in Wikipedia's Cast section of Guardians of the Galaxy (2014), of Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 2 (an expanded role: takes up Yondu's arrow), and reprises in Thor: Love and Thunder. The Love and Thunder credit is already in data/cast-overrides.json (line 133–136) as `{actor: "Sean Gunn", character: "Kraglin Obfonteri"}`; it survives into data/cast-raw.json under Love and Thunder with `fromOverride:true`, and then silently disappears. Vol. 1 and Vol. 2's raw casts hold 15 and 15 entries; both cap out before Sean Gunn's bullet in the Cast section. Fix: add "Kraglin Obfonteri": "Kraglin" to the CANON block in scripts/scrape-cast.mjs (around line 435), and either raise the raw-cast intake or add Vol. 1 and Vol. 2 Kraglin credits to overrides.

**2. Thanos is credited in Infinity War and Endgame only; Guardians (2014) and Age of Ultron are missing.** Guardians of the Galaxy's Cast section: "Josh Brolin appears, uncredited, as Thanos through voice acting and performance capture" for the sit-down with Ronan mid-film; a full scene, not a stinger. Age of Ultron: "Josh Brolin makes an uncredited appearance during the mid-credits scene as Thanos" and, per the plot summary, "dons a metallic gauntlet ... and vows to retrieve the Infinity Stones himself." Both scenes are spoken. Under the speaking-cameo rule in data/cast-overrides.json.speaking_rule, both should be added to overrides. Fix: append `{"actor":"Josh Brolin","character":"Thanos"}` under "Guardians of the Galaxy" and "Avengers: Age of Ultron" in cast-overrides.json.

**3. Doctor Strange is missing from Thor: Ragnarok.** data/web-cast.json characters "Doctor Strange" lists five films (Doctor Strange, IW, Endgame, No Way Home, Multiverse of Madness). Wikipedia's Ragnarok Cast section: "Benedict Cumberbatch reprises his role as Dr. Stephen Strange from the film Doctor Strange (2016) in a brief appearance." Wikipedia calls it a "brief appearance," never "cameo"; Strange has dialogue with Thor and Loki. The parser did not pick it out of the "Additionally, ..." run-on paragraph. Fix: add `{"actor":"Benedict Cumberbatch","character":"Stephen Strange / Doctor Strange"}` to cast-overrides.json under "Thor: Ragnarok".

## Major

**4. Hulk and Captain Marvel are missing from Shang-Chi.** The film's Cast section: "Mark Ruffalo and Brie Larson appear, uncredited, in the mid-credits scene, reprising their respective MCU roles of Bruce Banner and Carol Danvers / Captain Marvel," and the plot: "Wong introduces Shang-Chi and Katy to Bruce Banner and Carol Danvers and the group discusses the origins of the rings." Both are spoken. Fix: add both to cast-overrides.json under "Shang-Chi and the Legend of the Ten Rings" (same shape as the Tony Stark / Incredible Hulk override already in the file).

**5. "Yukio" is one row for two different people who share a name.** data/web-cast.json characters "Yukio" (u=Fox) holds `actors:["Rila Fukushima","Shioli Kutsuna"]` and `films:["The Wolverine","Deadpool & Wolverine"]`. Rila Fukushima's Yukio in The Wolverine (2013) is Mariko Yashida's swordswoman companion; Shioli Kutsuna's Yukio, introduced in Deadpool 2 (Cast section: "Shioli Kutsuna stars as Yukio, Negasonic Teenage Warhead's girlfriend and fellow X-Man") and reprising in Deadpool & Wolverine, is a different character who happens to share the name. Wikipedia's Deadpool 2 note even calls it out: "Yukio previously appeared in The Wolverine, portrayed by Rila Fukushima" as a separate character. Same collision shape as Ant-Man (Hank Pym) vs Ant-Man, or Green Goblin vs Green Goblin (Harry Osborn). Fix: split into two rows, e.g. "Yukio (Yashida)" and "Yukio (Kutsuna)"; and add Deadpool 2 to the Kutsuna row (raw cast stopped at 11 entries and missed her).

**6. Kraglin Obfonteri override is silently discarded.** Root cause of finding 1, worth flagging on its own: the CANON dictionary in scripts/scrape-cast.mjs (line 435) contains `"Kraglin": "Kraglin"` but not `"Kraglin Obfonteri"`. lookup() tries the full string, then splits on "/" or " and "; "Kraglin Obfonteri" hits none. canonOf() returns null and the override drops on the floor with no warning. Any future override that names a mononym CODE_KEEP character by full name will fail the same way. Fix: either add every full-name variant to CANON alongside the mononym, or fall back to lookup() on the last whitespace-separated token when the whole string and all "/"-splits miss.

**7. Scraper cast cap is truncating supporting-cast bullets in dense pages.** data/cast-raw.json for Thor: Ragnarok carries 12 entries and ends at Fandral; Cumberbatch's Strange bullet is beyond that cutoff. Guardians Vol. 2 holds 15 and stops before Sean Gunn's Kraglin bullet. Age of Ultron holds 20 and ends just before the Josh Brolin bullet. Every finding above chases this same tail. Fix: raise the intake limit in scripts/scrape-cast.mjs, or run a second pass that reads the "Additionally, ..." tail paragraph for "reprises his role as / appears as / stars as" patterns (the parser already does this for the reprisal cue in prose; extend it to the tail-of-bulletlist paragraph).

## Minor

**8. "The Batman" (2022) sits in Elseworlds without a released theatrical sequel.** §14's scope rule requires "a shared universe, a trilogy, or a film with a theatrical sequel." The Batman: Part II is announced but unreleased; the Reevesverse has, on screen, exactly one film. Nolan's trilogy and Joker + Folie à Deux clear the bar; The Batman does not, unless "part of a multi-film continuity" is being read to include announced sequels. Editorial call, but the log's own precedent (the 2015 Fantastic Four pulled for being a one-and-done) argues for consistency.

**9. Superman's Kara (Milly Alcock, DCU) and The Flash's Kara (Sasha Calle, DCEU) are collapsed into one Supergirl row.** data/web-cast.json Supergirl: `actors:["Sasha Calle","Milly Alcock"]`, films across DCEU (The Flash) and DCU (Superman, Supergirl). The "recast is one character" rule was written for within-continuity swaps (Norton to Ruffalo). Two Karas from two different rebooted continuities is closer to the Green Goblin / New Goblin / Green Goblin (Harry Osborn) split the site already keeps, or the Silver Surfer / Silver Surfer (Shalla-Bal) split. Not clearly wrong (the DCEU and DCU are both "DC" and Kara is Kara in both), but not clearly right either; worth naming the choice in the code or splitting for consistency.

**10. Falcon and Captain America (Sam Wilson) are two rows for the same actor; that is correct per the mantle rule and the check confirms it.** Anthony Mackie's Sam Wilson is Falcon in six films through Endgame, then Captain America from Brave New World; the two rows share `real=Sam Wilson` and would look like a merge candidate to any fuzzy matcher. Same shape as Black Widow / Black Widow (Yelena), Falcon / Falcon (Joaquin Torres), Wasp / Wasp (Janet), Captain Marvel / Captain Marvel (Rambeau), Black Panther / Black Panther (Shuri). All five pairs share a name and are different characters, and all five are correctly kept apart. Noting it here so a future audit sweep does not helpfully "fix" the mantle by merging back.
