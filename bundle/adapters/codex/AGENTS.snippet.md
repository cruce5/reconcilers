## The Reconcilers

An audit team for this project: ten heroes with one lane each, a summoner, and a villain who checks their findings. It runs on demand only. Do not start it unless the analyst asks for it by name.

When they ask to run the Reconcilers episode, or for an audit before launch, read `reconcilers-episode.md` at the repo root and follow it exactly. The team is read-only: no edits, no commits, no deploys, and every report is written under `.reconcilers/`.
