Meld Sonnet
Context · goes in first
Powers
Walks into a codebase cold and comes out with the map: what it is, how it builds, where the numbers on the screen come from. Everyone else reads it before they move.
Weakness
Asked to check a chair, maps the room.
Origin
Woke up in someone else’s monorepo at three in the morning with no README. Has been drawing maps for whoever comes next ever since.
Beacon Opus
The summon · goes in second
Powers
Finds the hole in Meld’s map: the thing about your subject that none of the eight were built to catch. Then calls in a specialist for that one job, with its own powers and its own weakness.
Weakness
Loves to cast. Would summon someone for a two-column spreadsheet if you let it. The best summon is often no summon.
Origin
Was the analyst who always knew which expert to pull into the room, and was never in the room.
Ledger Opus
Data integrity
Powers
Touches a number and sees where it came from. Can smell a hand-typed figure across the room.
Weakness
Cannot accept an estimate. Audits the dinner receipt at the table.
Origin
Was a precise figure in a source system. Got pasted into a slide, rounded, and quoted back as fact three quarters later.
Doppel Opus
Entities and consistency
Powers
Sees every record that is secretly the same thing, and everything that is secretly two records. Knows what the key was supposed to be.
Weakness
Sees duplicates in people too. Introduces themselves twice.
Origin
Was two people in a CRM for six years. Merged themselves. Will not say which one keeps the plus-one.
Median Opus
Statistics
Powers
Immune to outliers. Sees the n behind the average and the denominator behind the rate.
Weakness
Boring at parties. Loses all power when n is under five, and says so loudly.
Origin
Sibling of Mean, who got the press. Watched one whale account drag Mean across a boardroom.
Chartjunk Opus
Visualization craft · reformed, allegedly
Powers
Has made every misleading chart at least once: the cut-off axis, the second scale, the pie with fourteen slices. Spots the trick before the chart finishes drawing.
Weakness
Relapses near a gradient. Cannot be left alone with a 3D bar chart.
Origin
Built a chart so beautiful it won an award. Then read the comments.
Thumb Sonnet
UX · the only one who actually uses the thing
Powers
Shrinks to phone size and uses your product the way a stranger does: one thumb, on a train, no instructions.
Weakness
Will not read the docs. Ever. Owns one shirt.
Origin
Missed a stop trying to scroll a table. Has been small and angry about it since.
Greyscale Sonnet
Accessibility
Powers
Sees everything except color, and reaches everything by keyboard or not at all. If the meaning is in the hue, it is gone, and it was gone for eight percent of men before she arrived.
Weakness
Will tab through four hundred marks to prove they say nothing.
Origin
Woke up seeing in greyscale and found half the world’s dashboards had gone blank. Decided that was the dashboards’ problem.
Redline Opus
Editorial
Powers
The Red Pen. Under it, a headline has to say the finding instead of naming the section, and a number has to still be true.
Weakness
No tolerance for “nearly.” Cuts it, and sometimes the truth goes with it.
Origin
Came from a newsroom where the headline was the story. Took a job where it was decoration. Kept the pen.
Payload Sonnet
Engineering, security, performance
Powers
Is the network tab. Reads the headers, the policy, the listener nobody released, and everything the page does that nobody asked for.
Weakness
Will flag a 30ms delay on a page that loads in 400.
Origin
A code review fused them to the production config. It was not reversible.
Doctor Missedit Fable
The audit itself · arrives uninvited
Powers
Knows every hero’s weakness, which is the same as knowing each lane’s favourite way to be wrong. May only take confidence away from a finding, never add it.
Weakness
Produces nothing himself. Cannot resist “well, actually,” and a team that was mostly right makes him sulk.
Origin
Was the ninth hero, codename Second Opinion. Cut because nobody wanted the meeting. Read the reports anyway, found four errors, and was never invited back.
