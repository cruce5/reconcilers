The Reconcilers

They don’t save the world. They check it.

By 🏴‍☠️ Bill Yost

Ten heroes find what your reviewers missed: a number that stopped tying, a label that lies, a chart that misleads by accident. One of them, Beacon, summons a specialist for your subject. Ten minutes, on your machine, free. Then Doctor Missedit turns up uninvited and picks apart their findings.

Get the team

Import it, or copy it in

The bundle is a Claude skill and eleven hero briefs. Nothing to compile, no account to make, no key to paste.

The easy way

Import the zip into Claude

  1. Download the bundle below.
  2. In Claude, open Customize, then Skills, then Import skill, and pick the zip.
  3. Open the folder you want audited and say /reconcilers.

Rides along with your account, so it is there in every project. The heroes travel inside the skill, so the team still runs in parallel.

Per project

Copy it into one repo

# from the folder that holds your product
bash /path/to/reconcilers/install.sh
& "C:path	o
econcilersinstall.ps1"

The installer only copies files: the skill to .claude/skills/reconcilers/, the eleven briefs to .claude/agents/. Copy them by hand if you would rather. Then run claude and say /reconcilers.

Somewhere else

Codex, Cursor, anything

bash /path/to/reconcilers/install.sh --platform codex

Writes an AGENTS.md that runs the whole episode in one agent. For Cursor, Aider, Cline or a shell script, the porting guide is one page.

Read-only. The team never edits, commits or deploys your product. Local. Nothing about it leaves your machine except through the AI model you were going to use anyway. Every report lands in .reconcilers/ beside your code, and fixing is a separate conversation that you start.

Meet the roster

Eight own a lane. Two go in first. One is a villain.

Meld reads the room, Beacon summons a specialist for your subject, and the rest work in parallel without comparing notes until the compiled report. Then Doctor Missedit shows up uninvited. Every hero runs on the model that suits its lane.

How an episode runs

Same Bat-time, same Bat-channel

It is meant to feel like calling a superhero team, and specifically like 1966. The title card names the episode after your product. Meld reads the room. Beacon reaches across the multiverse for the one specialist your subject needs, or stands down if the eight already cover it. The eight go in on one breath alongside the summon, each report lands with a sound effect, and the compiled report is ranked, deduplicated and cross-checked. Then: HOLD IT! A shadow falls across the Hall, and Doctor Missedit, who may only remove confidence from a finding and never add one, reads every report with each hero’s weakness in one hand and the evidence in the other.

House rules the theatre never overrides: only verified findings in the ranking, every finding cites a file and line or a screen, every report ends with what was checked clean, and no em dashes, which is a team quirk you should not fight.

Born on The Cape Index

They audited the site they were built on first

The team was assembled to check capeindex.com, a report on every Marvel and DC film since 2000. They found 78 things, 55 once the overlaps merged, and the Doctor found 7 places the fixes had missed. On a site built by the person who built them.

Other runtimes

Claude Code by default, not only

The bundle ships an adapter for Codex that runs the same episode in one agent, and a one-page porting guide for Cursor, Aider, Cline or a shell script. Standard roster is Sonnet and Opus; a flamethrower option puts every hero on Fable and tells you what it will cost in tokens first.
