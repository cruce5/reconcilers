The Reconcilers

They don’t save the world. They check it.

By 🏴‍☠️ Bill Yost

Ten superheroes read your dashboard, report or dataset before anyone else does. Each one checks a single thing: the numbers, the charts, the words, the phone screen, the wiring underneath. Ten minutes, free, on your machine. Then a villain turns up and argues with what they found.

Get the team

Import it, or copy it in

One zip: a Claude skill and eleven hero briefs. Nothing to compile, nothing to sign up for, no key to paste.

The easy way

Import the zip into Claude

  1. Download the bundle below.
  2. In Claude, open Customize, then Skills, then Import skill, and pick the zip.
  3. Open the folder you want audited and say /reconcilers.

Goes with your account, so the team is there in every project. The heroes ride along inside the skill.

In Claude Code

One command

/plugin marketplace add cruce5/reconcilers
/plugin install reconcilers@reconcilers

Skill and all eleven heroes at once, and it updates with the repo. Then say /reconcilers.

Per project

Copy it into one repo

# from the folder that holds your product
bash /path/to/reconcilers/install.sh
& "C:path	o
econcilersinstall.ps1"

The installer only copies files: the skill to .claude/skills/reconcilers/, the briefs to .claude/agents/. Do it by hand if you would rather. Then say /reconcilers.

Somewhere else

Codex, Cursor, anything

bash /path/to/reconcilers/install.sh --platform codex

Drops the episode next to your code and adds five lines to AGENTS.md pointing at it, so it stays out of the way until you ask. Works in Codex, and in Claude Code where it reads AGENTS.md too. For Cursor, Aider or a shell script, the porting guide is one page.

They only read. Nobody edits, commits or deploys your work. Reports land in .reconcilers/ next to it, and fixing anything is a separate conversation you start. Nothing leaves your machine except through the model you were already using.

Meet the roster

Eight own a lane. Two go in first. One is a villain.

Meld maps the place. Beacon calls in a specialist for your subject. The eight go in at once and never compare notes, which is the point: nobody talks anybody out of a finding. Then the Doctor arrives.

How an episode runs

Same Bat-time, same Bat-channel

It is built to feel like calling a superhero team, and specifically like 1966. A title card names the episode after your product. Meld reads the room. Beacon reaches across the multiverse, or stands down if the eight already have you covered. Reports land one by one with a sound effect. Then: HOLD IT! A shadow falls across the Hall, and Doctor Missedit starts taking the findings apart.

House rules nobody gets to override: a finding only makes the list if it was checked, every one of them points at a file and line or a screen, and each report ends with what turned out to be fine. Also no em dashes, which is a team quirk you should not fight.

Born on The Cape Index

They audited their own house first

I built them to check capeindex.com, a report on every Marvel and DC film since 2000. They found plenty. Then the Doctor found the places my fixes had missed, which was worse.

Other runtimes

Claude Code by default, not only

There is an adapter for Codex that runs the same episode in one agent, and a one-page porting guide for Cursor, Aider or a shell script. Standard roster is Sonnet and Opus. There is also a flamethrower option that puts every hero on Fable and tells you the bill first.
